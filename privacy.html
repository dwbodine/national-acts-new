Introduction

National Acts, Inc. ("National Acts," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information in connection with our website, ticketing services, and VIP experiences (collectively, the "Services").

By using the Services, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Billing address
  • Payment-related information processed securely by third-party payment processors

Transaction Information

  • Ticket and VIP package purchases
  • Event attendance records
  • Order history and receipts

Technical Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type and device information
  • Website usage data collected through cookies or similar technologies

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Process ticket and VIP package purchases
  • Deliver tickets, credentials, and event-related communications
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Facilitate VIP experiences and event operations
  • Improve and secure the Services
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

Email Communications

By purchasing through the Services, you may receive transactional communications related to your order, event, or VIP experience. You may also receive information about upcoming events and experiences where permitted by law.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link included in those emails. Transactional communications necessary to fulfill purchases are not affected.

How We Share Information

We may share personal information only as reasonably necessary with:

Artists, Venues, and Event Partners

  • To fulfill tickets, credentials, VIP experiences, and event operations

Payment Processors

  • To securely process transactions and prevent fraud

Service Providers

  • Vendors who assist with website hosting, email delivery, analytics, or customer support

Legal or Regulatory Authorities

  • When required by law, subpoena, or legal process

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality
  • Analyze site usage and performance
  • Customize user experience

You may adjust browser settings to disable cookies, though some features of the Services may not function properly.

Data Security

National Acts takes reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. National Acts is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party sites. Use of those sites is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under the age of 13. National Acts does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to your personal data
  • Requesting correction or deletion of your information
  • Opting out of marketing communications

Requests may be submitted through the contact information provided on our website.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

National Acts reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting to the website. Continued use of the Services constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact National Acts through the contact information provided on our website.
